## Project Overview
The goal of this project is to provide leadership with data-driven insights and recommendations to assess how effectively the network is performing following its launch and determine how it should evolve over the coming months and years. The findings will support decisions related to network development, operational improvements, and commercial performance, while identifying underperforming areas, operational or customer anomalies, and profitable elements that can be leveraged to support sustainable growth.


## Project Background
Borne2Win is an EV charging network company providing public charging infrastructure. The company launched its network in Paris in March 2021, with the ambition of becoming a leading EV charging network in the city before expanding its footprint across France and other European markets.

As a newly launched network, Borne2Win needs to understand how effectively its charging infrastructure is capturing demand, generating revenue, and delivering a reliable charging experience. The first several months of operations therefore provide an opportunity to assess network performance, identify performance gaps, and determine where operational or commercial improvements may be required to support sustainable growth.


Reporting to the Chief Operating Officer, this analysis evaluates the network's post-launch performance and provides insights for cross-functional teams across Finance, Product, and Operations. The objective is to understand where the network is successfully capturing demand and creating value, as well as where performance constraints may be limiting revenue growth or service reliability.


The available EV charging data covers individual charging sessions recorded between March and December 2021, providing visibility into three key areas of network performance: 

**Revenue performance** — Tracking revenue trends and revenue contribution across EVSEs and geographic zones to understand where the network generates value.

**Demand & charging performance** — Analyzing charging activity and energy consumption across time, locations, EVSEs, and cable types to identify demand patterns, underperforming assets, and opportunities to improve network utilization.

**Operational reliability** — Evaluating charging session success and failure rates across the network to identify operational performance gaps and opportunities to improve the customer charging experience.

## Key Insights


### Monthly Performance Section
Borne2Win revenue growth activity increase steadily from April to July 2021, with revenue growing every month (+10%, +8,9%, +6,9% MoM). This gradual increase consistent with an early ramp-up of a newly deployed charging network in the city. Pandemic restrictions may also have influenced activity levels.

Activity declined between July and August 21, with revenue decreasing by 14.5% MoM. The decrease may be partly explained by seasonal effects (summer holidays, lower business activity) although other factors cannot be yet ruled out.

From September to December 2021, revenue growth accelerated sharply, with an 85,3% increase in September - followed by continued but more moderate growth in the following months ; +35.5% in October, +45.9% in November and +26.3% in December. This may reflect increasing network adoption, greater awareness of the service, and the easing of pandemic-related restrictions, while the progressive moderation in monthly growth suggests a transition toward a more established level of demand.
